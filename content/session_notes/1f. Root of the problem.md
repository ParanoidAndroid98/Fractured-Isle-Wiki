# Root of the Problem
Act 1, Session 6
Session date: 16/08
Location: Aurixis

Start in the Tenebris Reliquary, the armoury shop owned by Lady Seraphim

Lady Seraphim provides high quality items, but are very expensive. 
Provides Gylly a basic cloak as they are naked from slime encounter.
She offers that if Syrio won his first set of tournaments, she could loan a set of armour.

**[Side Quest]** Lady Seraphim can forge armours for us using rare materials, leathers, etc. and potentially keep some of the rare properties of the material.

She may be able to make a set of heavy armour for Syrio with the properties of light armour.

**To Cathedral**. Golden red building that appears to be carved out of the landscape. Vines running up the outside.
Cathedral is a shrine to Vitasol, father of creation. We go inside and are greeted by a priest. 
Syrio and Gylly go to huge tree in the center, Syrio to leave an offering, Gylly pours some of the magical liquid onto the tree but no evident effect.

Regroup with Priest in private chamber. Crystal/spikes are conduits for power of big tree to be harnessed.
A skilled archanist could make them, and could be used for bad harnessing.

A ?student/curate/young member of church? tells us about noises head under the cathedral.

**To Catacombs under cathedral**. Find a small room with coffins and enscriptions, 3 statues in the centre with basins.
Puzzle in which we had to put items in each of the basins. Ashes of dead in one, earth in another, water from the tree in the third.
Solving the puzzle opened a large door going into deeper areas of the catacombs.

Eventually come to a large room with beautiful murals, lots of passages. 3 large crystals, one altered with metal wrapped round it.
As we try and remove the metal from the altered crystal, noises are heard deep in a tunnel. 
We keep removing it and start a fight against a ?large tree/ent thing?. 
During the fight we remove the metal, destroy the 3 crystals, and the creature is turned passive. 

It leads us deeper into the catacombs to a room of sand, with a drawing of the city with markings.
It is unknown what the markings mean
