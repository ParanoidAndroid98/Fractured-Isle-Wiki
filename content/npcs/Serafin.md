## ✦ Who Is Serafin?

Serafin is a vampiric blacksmith and artisan of unparalleled talent, whose name is whispered with equal parts admiration and awe throughout Aurixis. They run the prestigious **Serafin Armours & Arms**, a magnificent establishment in the city's finest district where weapons and armour are treated not merely as tools, but as works of art.

Every piece that leaves Serafin's forge has been subjected to their exacting standards. Nothing is allowed to leave the workshop unfinished, imperfect, or merely *good enough*. The shop itself reflects this philosophy: dark polished wood, gilded metalwork, velvet displays and exquisite weapons surround customers, while beautifully decorated skeletons silently attend to the day-to-day running of the establishment.

Serafin's presence in Aurixis is remarkable for another reason. As a member of the **Sanguine House's Rolling Class**, they are a vampire of considerable status and power. It is rare to see one of such lineage and ability so far from the Necromantic Lands, and rarer still to find one who has devoted themselves so completely to the art of blacksmithing.

Whether this devotion was entirely their own choice is another question.

## ♜ Personality

Serafin is intelligent, calculating, and almost always several steps ahead of whoever they are speaking with. They possess the effortless confidence of someone who has spent a very long time mastering both their craft and themselves.

They are flirtatious in a deliberate rather than playful way. Serafin enjoys making people uncomfortable, particularly when they discover that their charm can be just as effective as intimidation. A lingering look, a quiet compliment, or a hand placed momentarily on someone's shoulder can all be weapons when wielded correctly.

Beneath the charm lies a deeply domineering personality. Serafin expects obedience from their servants and respect from their customers. They do not shout when they can simply lower their voice and make the room listen.

Above everything else, Serafin is proud.

Not merely of their vampire blood, but of what they have *made themselves into*.

> “You think talent is enough? No, no, no. Talent is only the beginning. The rest is discipline. Years of discipline.”

## 🔨 The Master Artisan

Serafin's greatest pride is not their lineage.

It is their craft.

They spent years learning the secrets of metallurgy, weapon construction, armour design and magical smithing. They studied techniques from masters across the world and refined them through decades of obsessive practice.

To Serafin, forging is almost a spiritual practice.

Metal must be understood before it can be shaped. A weapon must have purpose before it can have beauty. Armour must move with the person wearing it rather than merely sit upon them.

They will happily spend hours discussing the balance of a sword, the curvature of a breastplate or the precise temperature required to temper a particular alloy.

They become almost frighteningly animated when discussing their work.

> “Look at this edge. You see? The steel remembers every mistake you make. You cannot lie to metal, darling.”

## 🦴 The Staff of Serafin Armours & Arms

The shop is staffed entirely by skeletons.

Unlike the crude undead commonly associated with necromancers, Serafin's servants are beautiful works of art in their own right.

Their bones have been carefully polished and decorated with **intricate gold inlays, filigree and carved patterns**. Some possess golden fingertips. Others have ornate gold lines running along their skulls and rib cages.

They move silently through the shop, carrying weapons, adjusting displays, cleaning armour and assisting customers.

Each skeleton appears to have been individually crafted.

Serafin considers them part of the establishment's presentation.

> “Why should my assistants look like corpses? If they are to represent my name, they must have some dignity.”

## 🧛 Appearance

Serafin is tall and strikingly beautiful, with the unnaturally flawless features characteristic of a powerful vampire.

Their pale complexion contrasts sharply with dark hair, which is tied high and neatly away from the face while working. A few loose strands are allowed to fall around their cheeks, giving their otherwise immaculate appearance a deliberately careless quality.

Unlike the extravagant clothing often associated with vampire nobility, Serafin dresses primarily as a **working blacksmith**.

They wear a fitted fine-leather apron over a dark blouse with the sleeves rolled high above the elbows. Wide riding trousers allow freedom of movement while maintaining an elegant silhouette, tucked into tall polished leather boots.

Gold jewellery and small pieces of finely crafted metal decorate their outfit, but these are practical demonstrations of their own work rather than simple ornamentation.

Their hands are often stained with soot, oil and traces of metal.

Serafin considers this beautiful.

## 🗣 Voice & Mannerisms

Serafin speaks with a **low, refined Eastern European accent**, speaking slowly and deliberately as though every sentence has been considered before being spoken.

- Rarely raises their voice. The quieter they become, the more seriously people tend to listen.
- Frequently calls people **“darling,” “dear one,”** or **“my friend”**, whether they actually like them or not.
- Has a habit of tilting their head slightly when someone says something foolish.
- Runs a finger along a blade while discussing its quality, inspecting the edge with almost unsettling intimacy.
- When irritated, their accent becomes noticeably stronger and their sentences shorter.
- Often smiles immediately before delivering an insult.
- Uses silence deliberately, allowing other people to become uncomfortable before continuing.

### Sample Speech

> “Ah. You have come to buy sword.”

> “Please, darling. Do not touch that.”

> “You call this armour? No. No, no. This is *metal pretending to be armour*.”

> “You wish for something beautiful? Very well. But beauty without strength is only decoration.”

> “I have lived long enough to know this: everyone wants perfection. Very few are willing to suffer for it.”

## 🖤 Wants & Fears

### What They Want

Serafin wants to become something that exists beyond their vampiric lineage.

They have already achieved wealth, status and considerable power. None of these things truly satisfy them.

What they want is **mastery**.

They want their name to become synonymous with craftsmanship. They want weapons and armour created by their hand to be recognised centuries after they were made.

More than anything, Serafin wants to prove that their achievements are *theirs*.

Not the achievements of the Sanguine House.

Not the achievements of a vampire.

**Serafin's achievements.**

> “My blood gave me time. It did not give me skill.”

### What They Fear

Serafin fears mediocrity more than death.

The thought of spending centuries producing work that is merely adequate is almost unbearable to them. They would rather destroy a flawed masterpiece than allow it to carry their name.

There is also a deeper fear beneath their arrogance.

Serafin did not necessarily choose the path that brought them to Aurixis.

Their devotion to blacksmithing may have begun as an obligation, a punishment, an experiment, or an order from someone far more powerful.

They have never explained the full circumstances.

And they do not like being asked.

## ⚔ Serafin Armours & Arms

The shop itself is one of the most prestigious armouries in Aurixis.

It sits in an elegant part of the city far removed from the soot and grime of traditional smithing districts. From the outside, it resembles a noble townhouse or private gallery rather than a working forge.

Inside, however, the sound of hammering can be heard throughout the building.

The showroom is filled with:

- Ornate enchanted plate armour
- Intricately balanced swords
- Elegant duelling weapons
- Custom ceremonial weapons
- Magical shields
- Fine leatherwork
- Gold-inlaid weapons
- Armour commissioned by wealthy nobles
- Strange pieces whose origins Serafin refuses to discuss

The skeleton servants move silently between the displays, maintaining the shop with mechanical precision.

The forge itself lies behind the showroom.

Unlike the elegant front of the establishment, the forge is hot, dark and industrial.

This is where Serafin is most at home.

## ⚔ Role in the Campaign

### Prestige Armourer

The party can commission Serafin to create exceptionally powerful weapons and armour.

Their services are expensive, but the quality is extraordinary.

---

### Information Broker

Serafin has spent decades among Aurixis' nobility and wealthy elite.

They know who buys what, who owes whom money, which nobles are secretly enemies, and which families have skeletons hidden in their closets.

Sometimes literally.

---

### Powerful Contact

As a member of the Sanguine House, Serafin possesses connections far beyond Aurixis.

Earning their favour could open doors that would otherwise remain firmly closed.

---

### Potential Rival

Serafin is not automatically an ally.

If the party insults their craftsmanship, breaks one of their creations, or treats their work as mundane, they may earn themselves a very powerful enemy.

Serafin does not forgive insults to their art easily.

---

### Unusual Quest Giver

Serafin's requests are unlikely to be ordinary.

They may need rare metals, monster components, ancient weapons, forgotten forging techniques, or magical materials.

And if the party succeeds?

Serafin may reward them with something truly extraordinary.
