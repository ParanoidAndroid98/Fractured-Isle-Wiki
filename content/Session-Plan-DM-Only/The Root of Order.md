# The Root of Order
*D&D One-Shot Session Plan*

---

## 1. Beginning: In the University
* **The Hook:** Students are summoned by **Professor Aldren Vey**, who has handpicked them for a simple university study.
* **The Persona:** The professor appears charming, comforting, answers questions openly, and tells students that magical fluctuations have been reported throughout the city, disrupting the natural flow of magic through **Aurixis**.
* **The Mission:** The university has a duty to understand why and try to stabilize the system.
* **The Gear:** Students receive small arcane rings called **Oroburos Fields**, which can be installed at points of fluctuation to stabilize the fields.
* **The Location:** The professor directs them to check out the old trees of the city, especially the one at the heart of the merchant district.
* **The Warning:** A stern reminder not to tamper with the devices as they are experimental and new.

### **Player Checks (Devices)**
* **Arcana:** DC 14
* **Insight:** DC 15
* **Perception:** DC 15

---

## 2. To the Tree & Tavern
* **Location:** The *Root and Lantern Tavern*, which houses the very old tree mentioned by the professor.
* **NPCs:** Innkeepers **Mara and Ellion Voss**, who are quite dismissive of the students: *"Why are you here, didn't you get enough data beforehand, the tree is being even weirder."*
* **Insight (DC 14):** Reveals that Mara is scared and Ellion is a bit frightened.

### **Investigation Checks (Inn & Tree)**
* **Nature (DC 12):** The tree is healthy but stressed.
* **Arcana (DC 12):** Significant magical fluctuations.
* **Perception (DC 14):** Roots appear to move slightly.
* **Insight (DC 13):** Mara is very worried.

---

## 3. Installation of Oroburos Rings

### **First Anchor: Beneath the Garden**
* **Finding the Spot:** Use checks to locate the right position within the garden.
* **Activation:** Align the runes and activate the arcane device, which pushes a small spike into the ground from the ring.
* **Arcana Check:** DC 11 to use the device correctly.
* **Result:** Fluctuations drop and positive feedback is received.

### **Second Anchor: Into the Basement**
* **Finding the Spot:** Must make a check to find the correct location.
  * **Nature:** DC 12
  * **Investigation:** DC 12
* **Clue (Investigation 14+):** Someone has dug here already.
* **Activation:** Arcana DC 12 check.
* **Result:** Fluctuations reduce further and stabilize. An Arcana or Nature check (DC 14) reveals that magic is being redirected and flowing deeper into the city.

### **Third Anchor: The Roots**
* **Finding the Spot:** Nature DC 13 or Investigation DC 16 to find the correct installation location.
* **Activation:** As they install it, roots pull away from the magic. If installed anyway, the tree goes still, a *thump* echoes, and a pulse of yellow magic flows through the roots.

---

## 4. Investigate the Issue
* **Arcana (DC 12):** The anchors are stealing energy.
* **Nature (DC 13):** Energy comes from the tree.
* **Investigation (DC 13):** The anchors are not stabilizing, but converting.
* **History (DC 14):** The runes are incorrect and the spike is wrong; this is transmutation magic, not stabilization.
* **The Reveal:** The tree bends toward them and lets out pained whispers: *"Whyyyy, please help me, it hurts, it is control."*

---

## 5. The Choice
1. **Remove** the anchors to reverse the work.
2. **Leave** and investigate.
3. **Destroy** the anchors, risking backlash.
4. **Tell** the inn owners and ask for advice.
5. **Just leave.**

* **The Trigger:** As they make their choice, a small stone starts to glow on their back, and a calm, measured voice says: 
  > *"Do not touch the anchors, your work was done so perfectly, do not destroy it."*
  > *"I am afraid you have discovered too much, what a pity."*

---

## 6. The Confrontation
* **Arrival:** Professor Vey walks over and stands at the gate to the inn with **3 perfectly calm senior students**.
* **The Threat:** He calmly threatens the players.
* **The Turning Point:** The innkeepers come out pleading for them to stop. Without remorse or emotion, Professor Vey surgically strikes them down with magic: *"What a shame, but the work must carry on."* (The senior students do not even flinch).
* **The Ultimatum:** Disappointed, he offers them the choice to leave with him or face the consequences (encourage players toward the latter).

---

## 7. The Final Combat

### **Professor Vey**
* **HP:** 78 | **AC:** 18 | **Initiative:** +5 (Wisdom +4)
* **Abilities:**
  * **Arcane Bolt (+6, 60ft):** 1d10 + 4 force damage.
  * **Binding Sigil (60ft):** DEX 14 save or restrained.
  * **Order Pulse (Recharge 5-6):** DC 14 CON save or 2d6 force damage.
  * **Anchor Command (1/round):** Command anchors to pulse (1d6 force damage, 10ft), use restraining roots (DC 13 DEX), or stabilize (cannot be destroyed for 1-2 rounds).
  * **Reactions:** Reduce damage by 1d8 + 2; move 10ft without opportunity attacks if hit.
  * **Phase Shift:** Becomes more reckless as anchors are destroyed. If all are destroyed, channels magic into himself to gain **2 actions per round**.
  * **The Correction (Fate Ability, 1/round, 100ft):** Target makes a DC 15 WIS save.
    * *Failure:* Completely collapses, drained of all emotion (*"you don't need to be destroyed as you can be fixed"*).
    * *Success:* Feels a part of them is missing and takes 1d4 psychic damage.

### **Senior Students (x3)**
* **HP:** 18 | **AC:** 14
* **1. Arcane Enforcer:**
  * Arcane Staff (+4): 1d8 + 2 damage.
  * Binding Spell: DC 12 DEX save.
* **2. Duelist:**
  * Rapier (+4): 1d8 damage.
  * Parry Reaction: Reduce damage by 1d6 + 2.
* **3. Ritualist:**
  * Order Ray (+4, 60ft): 1d6 + 4 force damage.
  * Repair/Reinforce Anchor: Restoring 5 HP or bringing them back to 1 HP once each.

### **Anchors**
* **HP:** 14 | **AC:** 13
* **Disable / Stop via:**
  * Arcana DC 12
  * Nature DC 13
  * Strength DC 15

### **Environment**
* The tree starts creating **difficult terrain** as energy is released, getting angry at both sides and screaming.
