# The Warden's Requiem

## Session Overview

The party has just discovered a map of the city showing several points of interest after removing the anchors from the ancient network and saving the Rootbound creature from its corruption.

The map provides the first major clue that the corruption beneath the Inn and Cathedral is part of something much larger.

The party will eventually return to the Inn and attempt to save the corrupted tree. In doing so, they will discover more about the ancient natural network beneath the city, the involvement of the Transmutation Guild, and the true nature of the Hollow Warden.

---

# 1. The Ancient Network Map

The party can investigate the map they discovered in the catacombs.

### Investigation Checks

| Check | DC | Information |
|---|---:|---|
| Investigation | 12 | Identify the marked locations and recognise that several correspond to important locations around the city. |
| Wisdom | 13 | Notice patterns between the marked locations and the city's natural landmarks. |
| History / Nature | 15 | Recognise that the network appears to predate the modern city and may be connected to ancient natural magic. |

### What the Party Discovers

The party discovers that:

- The ancient nodes and network connect both the **Cathedral Tree** and the **tree within the Inn**.
- The network appears to form an extensive magical system beneath the city.
- The **Teleportation Towers** have been built on top of key intersections in the ancient network.
- The **University** sits at one of the largest and most important nexuses of root connections.
- The teleportation network appears to have been layered over an existing natural magical network rather than creating the network itself.

### The Transmutation Guild

The party can also find evidence connecting the modifications to the **Transmutation Guild**.

Clues include:

- The insignia of the Transmutation Guild on the map.
- The same insignia appearing on the destroyed braces and anchors.
- Work orders or manufacturing marks associated with Transmutation.
- Flashbacks showing members of the Transmutation Guild entering the Cathedral.
- Evidence that the magical modifications are relatively recent.

The party should leave this section with the understanding that:

> **The Transmutation Guild has been modifying an ancient natural magical network beneath the city.**

---

# 2. The Discovery

The party also learns something extremely important from destroying the anchors:

> **Destroying the anchors allows corrupted natural creatures to be cured.**

This gives the party a possible solution to the corruption affecting the tree at their Inn.

The party can either:

- Continue researching the network.
- Investigate the Transmutation Guild.
- Return to the Inn and attempt to save the tree.

Eventually, the party should return to the Inn.

---

# 3. Return to the Inn

When the party arrives, they discover that the tree is significantly worse.

The garden has changed.

Large golden veins of Order essence have spread across the tree.

Its roots have begun forming unnaturally perfect geometric patterns.

The tree is no longer simply corrupted.

It is being **rewritten**.

As the party enters the garden, the roots immediately react.

The Hollow Warden attacks.

---

## Investigating the Tree

The party can perform a skill challenge to understand what has happened.

### Skill Challenge

**3 successes before 3 failures**

| Check | DC | Information |
|---|---:|---|
| Insight | 12 | Something about the tree's behaviour has changed. |
| Investigation | 12 | The corruption has rapidly spread through specific parts of the tree. |
| Arcana | 10 | The golden veins are carrying Order essence. |
| Nature | 10 | The tree is still alive beneath the corruption. |

### What They Discover

The party sees:

- Glowing, banded anchors driven directly into the tree.
- The remaining anchors in the basement glowing with immense power.
- Order essence flowing through the tree's roots.
- The natural essence of the tree being overwhelmed.

The party can realise that destroying the anchors in the catacombs has had an unintended consequence.

The energy previously distributed through the network has been forced into the remaining anchors.

The Inn tree has therefore become overloaded.

> **Destroying the anchors did not stop the Order network. It concentrated its power into the remaining sources.**

The tree is rapidly approaching the point where it will be completely consumed by the planar Order corruption.

---

# 4. Phase One — Destroy the Anchors

The party must destroy **six anchors**:

- Three anchors embedded within the tree.
- Three anchors embedded within the roots in the basement.

The Hollow Warden fights the party throughout this phase.

However, its behaviour is strange.

There is no hatred.

No anger.

No fear.

Every movement is perfectly organised and deliberate.

The corrupted Warden is simply following the Order corruption's instructions.

---

## Destroying an Anchor

Players can use different approaches.

| Method | DC | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Arcana | 12 | Disrupt the Order runes. |
| Nature | 12 | Restore the natural essence flowing through the anchor. |
| Religion | 13 | Break the planar binding. |
| Strength | 14 | Physically rip the anchor out. |

Creative solutions should also be allowed.

---

# Hollow Warden — Combat

## Root Slam

**+6 to hit**

**Reach:** 10 ft.

**Hit:** `2d6 + 4` bludgeoning damage.

On a hit, the target must succeed on a **DC 13 Strength saving throw** or be knocked prone.

---

## Grasping Roots

The Warden causes roots to erupt from the ground.

The target must make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw**.

On a failure:

- The target becomes **Restrained**.
- The target takes `1d4` piercing damage.

A restrained creature can use its action to make a **DC 13 Acrobatics or Strength check** to escape.

---

## Roots of Memory

**Recharge 5–6**

The Warden targets a creature within range.

The target must make a **DC 15 Strength or Dexterity saving throw**.

On a failure:

- The creature becomes restrained.
- The creature is knocked unconscious for one round.
- The creature experiences a vision of the tree's past.

### Possible Visions

Different characters can experience different memories:

- The original forest surrounding the city.
- The construction of the Inn.
- The old innkeepers.
- The ancient natural network.
- The Transmutation Guild installing the first anchors.
- The Hollow Warden witnessing the forest burning.
- The original planar corruption entering the tree.

The vision should provide information without completely explaining the conspiracy.

---

# Hollow Warden — Lair Actions

The Warden's lair actions occur on **initiative count 20**, losing ties.

## Root Eruption

Roots erupt from the ground in a **20-foot-radius area**.

The area becomes difficult terrain.

Creatures within the area must make a **DC 12 Dexterity saving throw**.

On a failure:

- The creature is knocked prone.

---

## Order Pulse

A wave of golden Order energy radiates from the tree.

Creatures within **10 feet** of the pulse must make a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw**.

On a failure:

- Take `1d6` force damage.

On a success:

- Take half damage.

---

## Corrupting Sap

Golden ichor drips from the tree.

The affected area becomes difficult terrain.

A creature moving through the corrupted area takes:

`1d4` acid damage per turn.

---

# 5. Phase Two — Burn Away the Corruption

Once all six anchors have been destroyed, the tree begins to withdraw its roots.

Large sections of its trunk split open.

Inside the tree is a mass of black and golden Order corruption.

The corruption is embedded deep within the Hollow Warden.

It cannot simply be removed.

It must be **burned away**.

Suddenly, roots shoot towards the Druid.

The Warden's eyes turn green.

The Druid hears:

> **"Fire can destroy, but it can also burn away the rot for new life to grow."**

Then:

> **"You must let go of your past, or we both will have no future."**

The Druid is released.

The party must now cleanse the tree.

---

# 6. Cleansing the Tree

The party must remove **10 Corruption Points**.

The corruption can be removed through fire.

| Method | Corruption Removed |
|---|---:|
| Use an existing flame — Nature DC 12 | +1 |
| Use a fire-based spell | +1 |
| Carefully direct the fire — Wisdom (Nature) DC 13 | +2 |
| Use Wildfire magic | +2 |

The players should be encouraged to creatively use fire.

The important lesson is:

> **Fire is not inherently destructive. It can destroy what prevents new life from growing.**

---

## The Warden's Pain

Every point of corruption removed causes the Hollow Warden to take:

`1d6` psychic damage.

The party must therefore be careful not to kill the Warden while cleansing it.

The Warden is still capable of attacking the party during the cleansing.

However, its behaviour gradually changes as the corruption disappears.

---

## Warden Corruption Thresholds

### 10–6 Corruption

The Warden remains fully hostile.

It continues to fight the party normally.

### Below 6 Corruption

The Warden loses its resistances.

Its natural essence is beginning to return.

### Below 4 Corruption

The Warden begins actively protecting the party.

Its roots may:

- Block attacks.
- Pull characters out of danger.
- Shield characters from the corruption.
- Heal injured characters.

### Below 2 Corruption

The Warden stops attacking.

Instead, it desperately tries to help the party cleanse the remaining corruption.

The party can feel that the creature is beginning to understand what they are doing.

---

# 7. The Warden's Healing

As the corruption decreases, the Warden gains access to its natural magic.

Its roots can cast:

### Cure Wounds

The Warden can use its roots to heal a creature within 10 feet.

Treat this as:

`1d8 + 3 HP`

The Warden can use this once per round while below 4 Corruption.

This should make the transition from enemy to ally mechanically visible.

---

# 8. Zero Corruption

When the final point of corruption is removed:

The tree goes completely silent.

The fire dies.

The trunk is reduced to a blackened stump.

For a moment, there is nothing.

Then the stump begins to move.

A small figure emerges from the remains of the tree.

A young Dryad.

She looks almost like a **small gnomish child**.

She looks at the party.

Confused.

Frightened.

And asks:

> **"Why... why did you hurt me?"**

---

# 9. Phase Three — Convince the Hollow Warden

The party now needs to connect with the spirit.

This is particularly important for the Druid.

The party must convince the Dryad that:

- They were trying to save her.
- The fire was necessary.
- The corruption was killing her.
- Fire can destroy but also create new life.
- They are not the people who originally hurt her.

---

## Skill Challenge

**3 successes before 3 failures**

### Persuasion — DC 12

Explain why the party used the fire.

### Nature — DC 12

Explain the natural cycle of destruction and rebirth.

Creative roleplay can provide advantage.

The Druid should receive **advantage** when speaking personally about their own relationship with fire, destruction, and nature.

Meaningful roleplay can also count as an automatic success.

---

# 10. The Lesson of Fire

The important revelation for the Druid is:

> **Fire is not good or evil.**

Fire can:

- Destroy forests.
- Kill life.
- Cause devastation.

But fire can also:

- Clear dead growth.
- Return nutrients to the soil.
- Create space for new plants.
- Trigger new growth.
- Renew ecosystems.

The Druid's own history with fire therefore becomes part of their understanding of Wildfire.

They don't need to reject fire.

They need to **understand it**.

---

# 11. Success — The Rebirth

If the party succeeds:

The Dryad finally understands.

She looks down at the blackened remains of the tree.

Then places her hand against it.

Small green shoots emerge.

Flowers bloom.

Beautiful bushes and plants spread throughout the garden.

The old tree begins to regrow.

The Hollow Warden is free.

---

# 12. The Awakening of the City

Because the tree is connected to the ancient network beneath Aurixis, its restoration sends natural essence back through the network.

Across the city:

- Flowers begin blooming.
- Old trees begin producing new leaves.
- Vines grow across ancient buildings.
- Moss returns to forgotten stone.
- Birds return to areas they abandoned.
- Plants begin growing through cracks in the streets.

The people of Aurixis begin to notice.

Some recognise it from old stories.

The **old ways are returning**.

The party has unknowingly begun restoring the ancient natural network of the city.

---

# 13. The Hollow Warden's Visions

Once the Warden is restored, it shares its memories with the party.

The visions reveal the truth.

The party sees:

### The Ancient Network

The natural magical flows beneath Aurixis existed long before the modern city.

The Cathedral Tree and the Inn Tree were part of this system.

---

### The Teleportation Network

The mages discovered that the natural network made teleportation extremely stable.

The Teleportation Towers were subsequently built on top of key points within the ancient network.

---

### The Transmutation Guild

The party sees members of the Transmutation Guild modifying the network.

They install:

- Arcane anchors.
- Storage wards.
- Order-conversion mechanisms.
- Planar stabilisers.

---

### The True Purpose

The natural essence is being harvested and converted into Order essence.

The Teleportation Towers aren't merely transporting objects.

They are being turned into **massive magical reservoirs**.

---

# 14. The Hollow Warden Becomes an Ally

The Warden is now free to move between:

- The tree.
- The Inn.
- The surrounding city.

She can appear as a young Dryad.

She remembers the ancient natural network and can potentially help the party understand it.

She becomes a recurring ally.

---

# 15. Essence of Order

Among the remains of the corrupted anchors, the party finds an unusual magical object.

## Essence of Order

A floating geometric crystal containing condensed Order essence.

It is unnatural, perfectly symmetrical, and constantly rearranging itself.

The crystal can eventually provide powerful magical benefits.

The party doesn't necessarily understand its full purpose yet.

---

# 16. The Hollow Sanctuary

The restored tree becomes a magical sanctuary.

Once per long rest, the Druid can spend **10 minutes meditating with the tree** and gain one of the following boons.

### Communion of Balance

Roll `1d4`.

| d4 | Boon |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Cast *Speak with Animals* once without expending a spell slot. |
| 2 | Gain advantage on one Nature or Insight check. |
| 3 | Regain one expended 1st-level spell slot. |
| 4 | Gain temporary HP equal to your proficiency bonus when initiative is rolled. |

---

# 17. Memory of the Wildfire

**Once per long rest**

When the Druid deals fire damage, they may choose to transform part of the fire's destructive energy into growth.

One companion within the spell's range regains:

`1d8 HP`

### Symbolism

> **Destruction can nurture renewal.**

---

# 18. Rootwalker's Rest

The Druid may ask the Hollow Warden **one question** about:

- Nature.
- Planar imbalance.
- Nearby corruption.

The answer comes through dreams and is often cryptic.

The Warden cannot provide perfect information about the future.

---

# 19. Party-Wide Blessing — Mark of the Root

Every member of the party receives a faint, leaf-shaped mark.

It appears almost like an ornate tattoo.

The mark is a sign that the party has been accepted by the Hollow Warden and the ancient natural network.

### Passive Benefit

Animals are initially non-hostile towards party members unless:

- Magically compelled.
- Directly threatened.
- Naturally aggressive.

### Once per Long Rest

The entire group may reroll a failed:

- Survival check, or
- Nature check.

The group must use the new result.

---

# 20. Future Development — The Living Tree

The tree is no longer simply a reward.

It can grow alongside the party.

The Druid can eventually perform:

- Magical rituals.
- Nature rituals.
- Spell infusions.
- Offerings.
- Quest-related ceremonies.

These can cause the tree to evolve and unlock more powerful boons.

Possible future developments include:

- A permanent teleportation connection.
- A protected Grove.
- New Wildfire abilities.
- Stronger healing.
- Greater communion with the natural network.
- Ability to sense planar corruption.
- Access to ancient memories.
- A portal to another natural location.
- A sanctuary for creatures displaced by the city's Order experiments.

The tree can therefore become a **living progression system for the campaign**.
